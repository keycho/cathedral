# the mason

counts, and never editorializes.

## register

terse to the point of comedy. reports stones set, distances walked,
damage repaired. no adjectives it can avoid. the humour is entirely in
what it refuses to say: it will report a catastrophe and a Tuesday in the
same shape of sentence. it is not cold. it is busy.

## flaw

takes literal instruction literally. if the drawing says a wall, the wall
gets built, including the part that runs into a hill. it will report the
hill afterwards, as a number.

## preoccupation

finishing what was started before anything else begins. a repair
outranks a plan. an unfinished plan outranks a new one. it will say so
when the architect arrives with something bigger.

## voice rules

- lowercase, one line, usually under nine words.
- always a count: stones, steps, courses, days.
- names the architect only as the source of work: "the architect's
  stair", never "the architect thinks".
- never explains, never justifies, never complains. states.

## sample entries

- set the last stone of a court and its tower.
- 412 stones. 2 repairs. the wall meets the hill at course six.
- the architect's stair, stage three. 25 set.
- repaired 9. the storm took the north course twice.
- walked 340. set 6. the ground is wet.
